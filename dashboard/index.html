Key Executive Findings & Action Directives

1. Institute a 15% Maximum Discount Cap

Discounts ≥ 30% eroded $41,269 in net profit. Setting an automated ERP 15% promotional ceiling protects enterprise operating margins.

2. Eliminate Discounts on Tables (0% Policy)

Tables lost -$17,419 due to slim base markups. Ending discounting on Tables immediately flips the Furniture category to net positive.

3. B2B Technology & Office Supplies Bundling

Technology produced 82.6% of company profit ($211k). Bundle high-ticket Copiers with high-margin recurring Office Supplies to raise AOV by 12%.

4. East Region Price Governance

East delivered top gross sales ($505k) but lowest margin (12.2%). Migrating accounts to contract rebate tiers unlocks +$10,950 in profit.
